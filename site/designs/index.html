One career, four designs

The same facts about Ben Fung, told four very different ways. Each one is a full page; pick the one that fits the reader.

    What he does, six selected outcomes, his recent roles in full and his earlier career in three phases.

    Selected roles as departures on a split-flap board, newest first, with a boarding pass to email him.

    A highlight report: every role on a Gantt chart from 1997 to 2026, the measured results and the recommendation he made at Gatwick.

    His career as a transit line, with an interchange for the client assignments loop.
