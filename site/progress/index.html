Version 0: the seed page

At 13:06 the factory bot made this: the name Ben Fung, one line on 25 years in technology and 20 in delivery leadership, a paragraph about how the site was made, and links. No photo, no roles, no figures.

The first five designs

The keeper wrote five very different designs of the same career story, plus a gallery to compare them. These are the first versions, before any review.

Who the agents are

The keeper is the agent that builds and looks after the site, and it runs on Opus. The design agent, also on Opus, measures every page and scores how it looks. Five simulated users each try the site from a different seat: a recruiter on a phone, a CIO, a senior project manager peer, a user of accessibility tools, and a sceptical head of a project office who checks every line against the LinkedIn screenshots. They run on Sonnet. This reporter and the accountant run on Sonnet too. Simulated users never replace real people.

What the keeper changed in answer

  • Facts: the infrastructure background is undated everywhere, the £1m is described as spend avoided after Ben presented the options, caveats are back on every figure, and the broadsheet has four roles and no invented quotation.
  • Phones: the departures table no longer clips, the programme plan chart was rebuilt to fit, and every page is 390px wide with no sideways scroll.
  • Looks: an "At a glance" strip on the home page, a line diagram and its own palette for the line map, an animated name board (off when reduced motion is set), a halftone portrait on the broadsheet, and a share card for each design.
  • Not done, because there is no source: a LinkedIn link, an availability date, rates and references.

What the keeper changed in answer

  • Facts: wording tied back to the profile, such as "client assignment for HX (Hurtigruten Expeditions)", "Figures from his profile" instead of "Benefits register", "London and one other location", and "about 20 in project management and delivery leadership" everywhere.
  • Every page now has its own preview description as well as its own share card.
  • Departures board: in light mode the dark board sits on a light page, and on phones each row is a two-row grid.
  • Line map: station cards in a three-column grid under the diagram on desktop, and larger diagram text.
  • Programme plan: a calmer navy and steel palette, a minimum bar width and larger labels.
  • Career path: a tighter phone layout. Broadsheet: balanced pull text and a fixed footer rule.
  • Gallery: real screenshot thumbnails of each design.
  • Not done, because there is no source: a LinkedIn link, an availability date, rates, references and an outcome for the easyJet work.

The sceptic still would not show the five designs to a friend, because five designs of one CV can read as a showcase of the pattern.

What the keeper changed in answer

  • Facts: share cards and page descriptions now say "more than 25 years in technology, about 20 in project management and delivery leadership", the plan's column is "Result", and the broadsheet marks LSEG as listed with his recent assignments.
  • Dinis Cruz is named on this page as the site's owner, as he is in the repository's own policy files.
  • Design: the line map's station cards no longer stretch or carry a half ring, the home stats have no empty cell on phones, Gantt labels are wider, the departures name is larger on phones and broadsheet columns keep headings with their text.
  • Accessibility: the departures phone table keeps a visually hidden header row, and the split-flap name starts at once.
  • Every design now ends with a large "Email Ben" button, which the recruiter asked for.
  • Gallery thumbnails were cropped below the switcher, and the programme plan share card was redrawn.

Agent time against wall-clock

  • From the seed commit at 13:06 to the last ledger entry at 15:36 is 2 hours 29 minutes 41 seconds (8,981 seconds). About 1 hour 59 minutes of that passed before the keeper started, with no recorded activity.
  • The keeper was active for about 31 minutes, from about 15:05.
  • The 19 recorded runs add up to 1,522.7 seconds, about 25 minutes. The three review rounds took about 3, 4 and 3 minutes of wall-clock. Agent time is larger because the reviewers in each round ran in parallel.

Not measured

  • Keeper (main agent, Opus) tokens: not in the ledger; harness cumulative figures are given separately under keeper_harness_usage, on a different basis, and are not added to totals.
  • Accountant (this run, Sonnet): tokens, tool uses and seconds not measured, because the harness reports them only after the run ends.
  • Tool uses not measured for all round 2 and round 3 simulated users and for the round 3 design agent (ledger gives tokens and seconds only). Round 2 design (18) and reporter (13) are recorded.
  • Per-agent token split between input and output is not measured (ledger gives one subagent_tokens figure per run).
  • Keeper's own elapsed time is only approximate: the ledger says '~15:05' for its start; its hours of thinking time versus waiting is not measured.
  • Time between the seed commit (13:06) and the keeper start (~15:05) is not measured as work: it is elapsed time with no recorded activity.
  • Reporter run timing is placed in round 2 from its 15:31 ledger entry; it was not part of a review round.
  • Money cost: not measured and not stated (tokens, not money).
  • Time spent by Dinis Cruz (providing materials, reading) is not measured.
  • The tokens and time of this reporter's final update are not measured either.
